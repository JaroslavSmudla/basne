Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to make our website better, more personalized, and to understand how visitors interact with our content. By using our website, you acknowledge that we use cookies as described in this policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your computer or mobile phone by your web browser. They help websites remember things about you to make your browsing experience smoother. You can learn more about them at www.allaboutcookies.org.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Necessary Cookies: These are essential for the basic technical function of our website. They do not collect any personal data and do not require your prior consent.
  • Performance & Analytical Cookies: These help us understand how visitors use our website (e.g., which pages are popular, how much time people spend on the site). We only use these cookies if you give us your explicit consent.

Specific Technologies Used on This Website

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We use Google Analytics to analyze web traffic and improve our presentation. Google Analytics places cookies (such as _ga) in your browser to distinguish unique users and analyze sessions. By default, we have enabled IP anonymization, meaning your IP address is truncated within the EU before being sent to Google servers. You can read more about how Google handles this data in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your Consent and Managing Cookies

In accordance with GDPR and EU cookie laws, performance and analytical cookies will never be stored on your device before you grant your explicit consent through our website's Cookie Banner.

You can change your preferences or revoke your consent at any time via the Cookie Banner, or you can configure your browser to block all cookies entirely through its settings. To opt-out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, you can install this tool: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies in Our Games and Mobile Apps

Please note that our mobile games (distributed via Google Play) and desktop games (distributed via Steam) do not use standard browser cookies. Technologies used within our mobile apps for advertising (such as Google AdMob) and telemetry (Unity Analytics) utilize mobile device identifiers and local application storage instead. These are fully disclosed and governed by our main Privacy Policy.
