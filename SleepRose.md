## Sleeping Beauty

### SL 1:
A beautiful day when she was born,  
the birds were singing, roses bloomed.  
Everything has a dark side,     
a figure clad in a cloak of red,       
cursed Sleeping Beauty to her bed.  
All the hopes today died.
